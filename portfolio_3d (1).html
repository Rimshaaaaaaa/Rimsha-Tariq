Pardes Guard

AI-powered travel safety platform

A multilingual platform that helps first-time travellers going abroad for work or study spot fraud, check documents for warning signs, understand travel requirements, and get support when things go wrong, such as unpaid wages.

  • Document analysis. Gemini extracts information from documents and flags potential warning signs.
  • Explainable risk assessment. A deterministic 22-rule engine returns STOP, CHECK, or LOOKS OK, with reasons and sources.
  • Journey planning. Step-by-step guidance, document requirements, official links, and deadline support.
  • Voice assistant. Multilingual conversation and interview practice using Gemini Live.
  • Agent lookup and case support. Review licensing information and warnings, prepare case letters, and organise evidence.
  • Accessibility and privacy. Multilingual support, voice interaction, selected offline capabilities, and user-controlled data sharing.

Contributed to building the solution, combining generative AI with rule-based decision-making, multilingual accessibility, document analysis, and Google Cloud services.
